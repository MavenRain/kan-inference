The second foundation increment constructs a sequence colimit and derives an initial algebra for the recursive signature `1 + X`. The concrete result assumes neither initiality nor a pre-existing natural-number recursor for the target carrier. It is a semantic construction in Lean's metatheory, not yet an internal derivation from Kanon's closed shape grammar.

The subsequent [indexed construction](INDEXED-CONSTRUCTION.md) extends preservation to arbitrary nullary/unary indexed signatures and constructs the vector model. General finite branching and the internal syntax connection remain open.

The construction has three reusable parts:

| Module | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `meta/KanonMeta/InitialChain.lean` | For an indexed polynomial, a colimit of its initial sequence plus preservation of that colimit gives an initial algebra, dependent elimination, and propositional beta. |
| `meta/KanonMeta/ChainColimit.lean` | Every sequence of indexed Type families has a constructed pointwise quotient colimit. `initialOfPreserves` specializes the initial-chain theorem to this construction, leaving preservation as its only hypothesis. |
| `meta/KanonMeta/NatConstruction.lean` | The Unit-indexed signature `1 + X` preserves every sequential colimit. Applying that proof to the constructed quotient colimit yields `recursiveInitial` without colimit or initiality hypotheses. |

The sequence is explicit: stage zero is the empty family, stage `n + 1` is the polynomial applied to stage `n`, and the connecting maps begin with the empty map and continue by polynomial mapping. A cocone records compatible stage maps. Its colimit record provides descent, factorization, and uniqueness for all target families in the same universe. Successor compatibility suffices because all arrows of the ordinary omega chain are finite composites of successor arrows.

The quotient carrier consists of tagged elements `(n, x)` with each element identified with its image at stage `n + 1`. Lean's `Quot` supplies the equivalence closure of these adjacent-stage identifications. Cocone compatibility proves that descent respects the relation; quotient induction proves uniqueness. No existence witness for this colimit is left as an argument.

The generic initial-algebra proof uses preservation to obtain the constructor map from shifted stage injections. Recursion on external stage numbers gives each target algebra a cocone. Descent supplies its fold. The preserved colimit's uniqueness proves constructor compatibility, and induction on stages proves uniqueness of algebra maps. These are the steps of the initial-chain theorem of [Adamek](https://dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/105583), also stated as Theorem 1.1 by [Pitts and Steenkamp](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~amp12/papers/coniau/coniau.pdf).

For `1 + X`, the nullary constructor's cocone legs agree along the sequence. Restricting the unary constructor to its single recursive argument produces an ordinary cocone on the original sequence. These two facts prove preservation directly, using only the supplied base colimit universal property. `NatConstruction.recursiveInitial` combines that preservation proof with the quotient construction. The previous `test/Initiality.lean` natural-number initiality witness is not imported or used.

All three modules are exported by `KanonMeta`. The generic sequence and polynomial construction aligns indices, shapes, positions, carriers, and displayed fibres at `Type u`. The concrete natural-number signature is Unit-indexed at `Type 0`. Constructor equations and dependent beta remain propositional equalities; no new judgmental reduction rule is introduced.

This is related to Kan extensions in a precise but currently external sense: a colimit is a left Kan extension along the unique functor to a terminal category. The dependency `comp-cat-theory` already provides that general correspondence. The new sequence records specialize the colimit universal property directly; no categorical adapter to that library's `LeftKanExtension` record is included in this increment. [Riehl, chapter 6](https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf)

The remaining primitive boundary is explicit. Stage indexing and iteration use Lean's natural-number recursion; the pointwise construction uses Lean's quotients and equality. The proof does not establish that Kanon's admitted SPi/SColl/SPar/SMu/SNu grammar can express this countable diagram without circularly using the SMu induction being justified. An internal account must either admit the appropriate diagrams or derive their colimits from other admitted Kan constructions. Countable coproducts and a coequalizer are a possible route; a new shape constructor is not mathematically forced. [Riehl, Theorem 3.5.11](https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf)

Preservation is not automatic for arbitrary recursive positions. The current vector signature has one recursive position per successor constructor; its Nat payload belongs to the constructor shape. General preservation for finite-position indexed polynomials is the next useful library theorem. For infinite branching, a signature such as `1 + X^Nat` can combine children of unbounded finite depth into a tree outside all finite stages, so the omega construction is insufficient in general. Lean parity must retain this distinction and address larger diagrams or other justified constructions. [Gambino and Kock, section 1.19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.4931), [Pitts and Steenkamp](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~amp12/papers/coniau/coniau.pdf)
